FAQ

Questions doctors ask us

What does the doctor need to provide?

A small space for the counter and a recommendation to patients. Everything else is handled by Spectacle Plus.

How does the doctor earn?

The doctor receives a share of the sales made from the counter. The terms are explained in person and agreed in a simple written agreement.

Who manages stock and billing?

We do. Frames, lenses, inventory, staff and billing are all managed by Spectacle Plus.

What do you sell?

Frames, lenses, sunglasses and contact lenses, for every need and budget.
